What it does

What a home mechanic actually reaches for.

🧠

AI Cortex

Describes the likely root cause of your codes with a probability and a next step, using Groq-hosted models and your own key.

🏛️

NHTSA recalls

Looks up open recalls and owner complaints for the vehicle from the public NHTSA database.

🏷️

NFC component tags

Tap a tag on the air filter, battery or belt to read or write its part number and install date. Android + Chrome.

📄

Printable reports

One tap makes a clean diagnostic report you can save as PDF or hand to a shop.

🎨

Four themes

Garage Day is the default for bright shops. Diagnostic Green, Midnight Blue and Amber Forge are there for night work.

📴

Installs, works offline

Add it to your home screen. The app shell is cached, so it opens without signal.

How it works

Plug in, pair, read.

Add your key

Paste a free Groq key in Config. It stays on your device.

Plug in

Fit a Bluetooth LE OBD-II adapter and turn the ignition to ON.

Scan

Tap Scan for Adapter and choose it from the browser's list.

Read

Review codes, ask the AI, check recalls, print the report.

Honest status: beta

Recall lookup, AI analysis, NFC tags, themes and reports are working features. The live link to the car's computer is still being tested on real hardware, so for now the dashboard shows demo data (a sample vehicle and simulated gauges) when no adapter responds.
